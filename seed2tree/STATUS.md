# fashion outfit search: project brief

## Right now
Paused. The agent stopped this project after checking the competition (see below). Nothing is being worked on unless you overrule it.

## The idea
While shopping, you ask "how would this look with my jorts?" and get back real photos of people wearing outfits that include that item, through Siri or the iPhone app.

## What works today
- An iPhone app that searches about 10,000 real outfit photos (from Reddit, Pinterest and wear.jp) using a photo of an item plus a line of text.
- The search runs on a server in the cloud (Modal), and the app talks to it end to end.
- It's good at matching the item, but often ignores the second half of a request (e.g. "with white sneakers").

## Why it was stopped
- **Pinterest already did this exact search** (photo of an item + "outfits for the beach") back in 2017, using photos it has the rights to show. Google Lens, ChatGPT shopping and closet apps like Whering cover similar ground.
- **The photos can't be shown publicly.** None of the 20,000+ outfit photos have a licence, and they're of real, identifiable people. Licensed photo sources are mostly stock or product shots, which loses what made this special.
- **Quality is well short of the goal:** about half of results match, against a target of three quarters.
